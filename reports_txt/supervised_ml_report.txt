## Executive Summary
We implemented and evaluated six supervised machine learning models: **Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, SVM, Decision Tree, and KNN**. 

**Key Findings:**
1.  **Accuracy Champion**: **LightGBM** and **XGBoost** achieved the highest accuracy (**99.89%** and **99.88%**) with extremely low inference latency, making them ideal for high-throughput clean environments.
2.  **Robustness Champion**: **KNN** proved to be the most resilient to noise, maintaining **94.4% accuracy** even under heavy noise (0.2 std dev), whereas tree-based models collapsed to <60%.
3.  **IoT Trade-off**: There is a clear trade-off between **Latency** (Tree models are fast) and **Robustness** (Distance models like KNN are stable but slower).

## 2. Robustness to Noise (Wireless IoT Scenario)
Wireless IoT links are prone to noise. We simulated this by adding Gaussian noise to the test features.

| Noise Level | KNN Acc | SVM Acc | RF Acc | XGBoost Acc | LightGBM Acc |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clean (0.0)** | 99.7% | 92.0% | 99.9% | 99.9% | 99.9% |
| **Low (0.01)** | **99.6%** | **92.0%** | 67.6% | 56.2% | 52.5% |
| **Med (0.05)** | **98.7%** | **92.1%** | 62.0% | 50.2% | 36.3% |
| **High (0.10)** | **97.1%** | **92.0%** | 59.0% | 43.0% | 31.7% |
| **Severe (0.20)** | **94.4%** | **91.6%** | 53.1% | 34.1% | 28.5% |

**Insight**: Tree-based models (RF, XGB, LGBM) rely on precise feature thresholds. Noise causes values to jump these thresholds, leading to catastrophic failure. Distance-based models (KNN, SVM) are inherently smoothing and handle noise much better.

## 3. IoT Performance Metrics
- **Latency**: **Decision Tree** is the fastest (0.0006 ms), suitable for ultra-low-power microcontrollers. **KNN** is the slowest (3.6 ms), potentially introducing bottlenecks.
- **Memory Footprint**: **Decision Tree** is tiny (0.3 MB). **KNN** requires storing the entire dataset (154 MB), which is prohibitive for edge devices.

## 4. Training Algorithm Pseudo-code
```python
FUNCTION Train_Supervised_Models(Train_Set, Test_Set):
    Models = [RandomForest, XGBoost, LightGBM, SVM, KNN, DecisionTree]
    Results = []
    
    FOR Model IN Models:
        # A. Training
        Start_Timer()
        Model.Fit(Train_Set.X, Train_Set.y)
        Train_Time = Stop_Timer()
        
        # B. Inference Evaluation
        Start_Timer()
        Predictions = Model.Predict(Test_Set.X)
        Latency = Stop_Timer() / Count(Test_Set)
        
        # C. Robustness Testing (Noise Injection)
        Noise_Levels = [0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2]
        FOR Level IN Noise_Levels:
            Noisy_X = Test_Set.X + GaussianNoise(std=Level)
            Noisy_Acc = Accuracy(Model.Predict(Noisy_X), Test_Set.y)
            Log(Model, Level, Noisy_Acc)
            
    RETURN Results
END FUNCTION
```

## 5. Discussion & Recommendation
- For **Gateways/Edge Servers** with stable connections: **LightGBM** or **XGBoost** are recommended for their superior accuracy and speed.
- For **Noisy Wireless Links**: **KNN** is robust but memory-heavy. A hybrid approach or training the tree models with noise augmentation is suggested to improve their robustness.
